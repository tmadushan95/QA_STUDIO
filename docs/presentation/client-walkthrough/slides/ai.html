Page · AI Assistant

AI that drafts, people approve

1

Generate test cases

Describe a feature, attach files, pick coverage: Smoke, Standard or Thorough. A checklist covers functional, negative, permission, validation, boundary, UI and accessibility.

2

Write steps

Drafts runnable steps for cases that have none. Anything it cannot automate is listed as “Check by hand”.

3

Teach it your app

Reads pages of the app, read-only, so its steps use the app’s real labels.

Guardrails, always on

AI never changes a test case · drafts need a person’s approval · only known step types · passwords and keys never leave the machine · spending limits per task.

AI is optional and the product works fully without it. Providers: Anthropic and Google Gemini, chosen in settings. Generate: the coverage bar shows what is covered and what is not (for example “Permission 1 · Not covered: Boundary”), so gaps are visible before saving. Saved Skills (reusable instructions, used as /skill-name) shape wording and scope, never the safety rules. Proposals are kept until saved or discarded, even if you switch pages. The guardrails are the most important part for a client: • AI only produces drafts; a person approves before anything runs. • Every AI step is checked against the same list of known actions as a hand-written step. • Secrets are removed from everything sent to a model. • There are spending limits on model calls. “Teach the assistant your app” blocks every write request in the browser, so reading a page can never change data.
