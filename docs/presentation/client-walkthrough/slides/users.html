Who it is for

Built for the whole delivery team

QA tester

Creates cases from Excel, AI or by hand, builds steps visually and runs them. No code.

Test lead

Organises projects, versions and suites, approves AI drafts, packages reusable custom steps.

Developer and DevOps

Triggers runs from Azure Pipelines, reads traces and screenshots to fix defects faster.

Manager and product owner

Reads the dashboard: pass rate, coverage, blocked cases and high-priority failures.

Business value

Less time on repetitive regression, wider coverage per release, faster feedback to developers, and one shared view of release readiness.

Map the personas to the client’s own people. Ask: “Who on your side would own the test library?” Value in one sentence per role: • Tester: automates without waiting for developers. • Lead: controls what runs, because AI only drafts and a person approves. • Developer: gets a reproducible failure with screenshots and a Playwright trace, not a vague bug report. • Manager: sees readiness on one page. Avoid promising specific time savings; we have no measured numbers for this client yet. Offer to measure them in a pilot.
