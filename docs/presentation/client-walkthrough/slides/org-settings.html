Organization settings

Execution

Browser and speed options: visible or headless, how many at once.

Pages

Named pages of the app, so a step can say “Go to the Draft projects page”.

AI

Provider and model, keys stored encrypted, spending limits.

Voice

On or off, push to talk or wake phrase, generator defaults.

Data and storage

Clear all data after typing CLEAR, with Undo and 7-day backups.

Audit log

Who changed what and when, across the whole platform.

Organisation-wide settings, usually owned by an admin or test lead. Two points to highlight for governance: • Even the most destructive action, clearing all data, takes a backup first. It can be undone straight away, or restored from Backups within 7 days. • The audit log records changes from every part of the system, which helps clients with compliance questions. Settings saved in the app always win over the starting values in configuration files.
