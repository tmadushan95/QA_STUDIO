This is where testers spend most of their day. Walk through the status chips left to right, because they tell the story of a case: it arrives with no steps, gets steps, may need fixing, waits for approval, and becomes ready to run. Only “Ready to run” cases move to Test Execution. Excel import is important for clients with existing test sheets: the system detects the columns and shows a preview before importing. Written steps such as “Click the Delete button” or “Verify ‘Saved’ is displayed” are converted into runnable steps automatically in the background. The converter never guesses: what it cannot understand is left for a person.
